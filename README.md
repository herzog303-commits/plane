# Rooftop plane watch

A daily dashboard of aircraft seen by a rooftop ADS-B receiver and a roof microphone: reception range, common tracks, aircraft types and classes, time of day, and wind and runway flow.

Everything here is a daily aggregate. It has no coordinates for the roof, no individual flights, no registrations, callsigns or owners, and no military or government aircraft. Track maps are in km east and north of the receiver and show a cell only where several different aircraft crossed it.

`index.html` and `data.json` are generated each day.
